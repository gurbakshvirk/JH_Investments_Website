About JH INVESTMENTS

A client-focused approach to financial distribution, tax-related assistance and financial education.

Who we are

Clear information. Considered decisions.

JH INVESTMENTS is based in Nabha, Punjab, India. We aim to help individuals and families better understand financial choices through approachable service, clear communication and attention to their stated needs.

Our work spans financial distribution, insurance planning, tax-related services, portfolio-related discussions and family financial education.

The exact services available to each client depend on the applicable registrations, authorisations, product-provider arrangements and regulatory requirements.
Our principles

How we aim to work

  • Respect for client goals and circumstances
  • Transparency about risks and limitations
  • Responsible handling of client information
  • Ongoing learning and service improvement
